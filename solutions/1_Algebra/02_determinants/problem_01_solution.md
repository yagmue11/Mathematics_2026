### Solution

**1. Computation of Determinants**

For the first matrix, the determinant is:

$$\det \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 5 & 4 \\ \end{pmatrix} = 3(4) - (-2)(5) = 12 + 10 = 22$$

For the second matrix, the determinant is:

$$\det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 2 & 14 \\ \end{pmatrix} = 1(14) - 7(2) = 14 - 14 = 0$$

**2. Invertibility**

A square matrix is invertible if and only if its determinant is non-zero.

The first matrix is invertible because its determinant is $22$, which is not zero.

The second matrix is not invertible (it is singular) because its determinant is exactly $0$.
