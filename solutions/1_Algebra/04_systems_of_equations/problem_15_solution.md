### Exercise 15. Classifying Three Systems

**System 1. One solution.** Adding the two equations gives $2x=2$, which fixes $x=1$. The equation $x-y=0$ then fixes $y=1$. The pair $(1,1)$ satisfies both equations.

**System 2. Infinitely many solutions.** Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $0=0$. The only remaining constraint is $x+y=2$, so every real choice of $x$ gives a solution with $y=2-x$.

**System 3. No solution.** Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $0=1$. This contradiction makes the system inconsistent.
