### Exercise 14. A Small Change in the Right-Hand Side

Adding the first two equations, which are the same in both systems, gives

$$
(x+y)+(2x-y)=2+1,
$$

so $3x=3$ and $x=1$. Substitution into $x+y=2$ gives $y=1$.

**First system.** The third equation, $3x=3$, follows from the first two. The system is consistent and has the unique solution $(1,1)$.

**Second system.** The first two equations still force $3x=3$, but the third equation requires $3x=3.1$. The system is inconsistent and has no solution.

The left side of the third equation is the sum of the first two left sides. For the system to be consistent, the same relation must hold on the right. Changing $3$ to $3.1$ breaks that equality.
