
# Matrix Operations

## Given

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\-1&3\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}4&-2\\0&5\end{pmatrix}
$$

Both matrices are 2 × 2, so addition and subtraction are possible.

## 1. Calculate A + B

Add the corresponding elements of both matrices.

$$
A+B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1+4 & 2+(-2)\\
-1+0 & 3+5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Answer:**

$$
\boxed{A+B=\begin{pmatrix}5&0\\-1&8\end{pmatrix}}
$$

## 2. Calculate A - B

Subtract the corresponding elements of B from A.

$$
A-B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1-4 & 2-(-2)\\
-1-0 & 3-5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Answer:**

$$
\boxed{A-B=\begin{pmatrix}-3&4\\-1&-2\end{pmatrix}}
$$

## 3. Calculate 3A - 2B

First, multiply every element of A by 3.

$$
3A=
\begin{pmatrix}
3&6\\
-3&9
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Next, multiply every element of B by 2.

$$
2B=
\begin{pmatrix}
8&-4\\
0&10
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Now subtract the corresponding elements.

$$
3A-2B=
\begin{pmatrix}
3-8 & 6-(-4)\\
-3-0 & 9-10
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Answer:**

$$
\boxed{3A-2B=\begin{pmatrix}-5&10\\-3&-1\end{pmatrix}}
$$

## 4. Why must matrices have the same size?

Matrix addition is only possible when both matrices have the same number of rows and columns.

This is because each element in one matrix must have a corresponding element in the other matrix.

For example, a 2 × 2 matrix cannot be added to a 2 × 3 matrix because their dimensions are different.
