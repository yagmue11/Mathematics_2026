### Exercise 1. Recognizing Curves from Canonical Form

Recognize the curves

$$x^2+y^2=9,$$

$$\frac{x^2}{16}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1,$$

$$y^2=8x,$$

$$\frac{x^2}{9}-\frac{y^2}{4}=1.$$

For each one, identify the most important features and make a sketch. Explain which parts of the equation let you recognize the type of curve.

### Solution

**1. Circle: $x^2 + y^2 = 9$**

- **Recognition:** Both variables are squared, appear on the same side with equal positive coefficients ($1$ and $1$), and sum to a positive constant $r^2 = 9$.
- **Features:** Center at the origin $(0,0)$, radius $r = 3$, and intercepts at $(\pm 3, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 3)$.
- **Sketch description:** A circle centered at $(0,0)$ passing through $(3,0)$, $(0,3)$, $(-3,0)$, and $(0,-3)$.

**2. Ellipse: $\frac{x^2}{16} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$**

- **Recognition:** Both variables are squared with positive signs on the left-hand side equal to $1$, but their denominators differ ($a^2 = 16 \neq b^2 = 9$), stretching the circle unequally along the coordinate axes.
- **Features:** Center at $(0,0)$, horizontal semiaxis $a = 4$, vertical semiaxis $b = 3$, vertices at $(\pm 4, 0)$, co-vertices at $(0, \pm 3)$, and focal distance $c = \sqrt{16 - 9} = \sqrt{7}$ with foci at $(\pm\sqrt{7}, 0)$.
- **Sketch description:** A closed oval centered at $(0,0)$, wider horizontally than vertically, inscribed inside the rectangle $[-4, 4] \times [-3, 3]$.

**3. Parabola: $y^2 = 8x$**

- **Recognition:** Only one variable is squared ($y^2$), while the other variable appears linearly ($8x$).
- **Features:** Vertex at $(0,0)$, axis of symmetry along the $x$-axis ($y = 0$), opening to the right ($x \ge 0$), focal parameter $2p = 8 \implies p = 4$, focus at $(2, 0)$, and directrix $x = -2$. Passing through $(2, \pm 4)$.
- **Sketch description:** A single smooth U-shaped branch starting at $(0,0)$ and opening rightward symmetrically across the $x$-axis.

**4. Hyperbola: $\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$**

- **Recognition:** Both variables are squared with opposite signs ($+$ for $x^2$, $-$ for $y^2$) equal to $1$.
- **Features:** Center at $(0,0)$, transverse semiaxis $a = 3$, conjugate semiaxis $b = 2$, vertices at $(\pm 3, 0)$ (no $y$-intercepts), focal distance $c = \sqrt{9 + 4} = \sqrt{13}$ with foci at $(\pm\sqrt{13}, 0)$, and asymptotes $y = \pm\frac{2}{3}x$.
- **Sketch description:** Two separate branches opening left from $(-3,0)$ and right from $(3,0)$, approaching the diagonal lines $y = \frac{2}{3}x$ and $y = -\frac{2}{3}x$ as $\vert{}x\vert{} \to \infty$.
