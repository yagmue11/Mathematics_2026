### Exercise 4. Parabola

For

$$
y^2=12x,
$$

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find points on the parabola for $x=3$ and use them to make a sketch.

### Solution

**1. Geometric features**

The equation has the canonical form $y^2 = 2px$ with $2p = 12$ ($p = 6$):

- **Vertex:** $(0, 0)$, since setting $x = 0$ gives $y = 0$ and $(0,0)$ is the extreme point of the curve.
- **Axis of symmetry:** The $x$-axis ($y = 0$), because replacing $y$ with $-y$ leaves $(-y)^2 = 12x$ unchanged.
- **Opening direction:** To the right (in the positive $x$-direction), because $y^2 \ge 0$ forces $12x \ge 0$, so $x \ge 0$.

**2. Points on the parabola for $x = 3$**

Substituting $x = 3$ into $y^2 = 12x$ gives

$$
y^2 = 12(3) = 36 \implies y = \pm 6.
$$

This yields two symmetric points on the curve:

$$
P_1 = (3, 6), \qquad P_2 = (3, -6).
$$

**3. Sketch description**

Plot the vertex at $(0,0)$ and the two guide points $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$. Draw a smooth U-shaped curve starting vertically tangent to the $y$-axis at $(0,0)$, opening rightward, and passing symmetrically through $(3,6)$ above the $x$-axis and $(3,-6)$ below it.
